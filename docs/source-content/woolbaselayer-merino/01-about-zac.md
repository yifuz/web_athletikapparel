# About ZAC - Merino Wool Apparel Manufacturer

Zhangjiagang Athletik Clothing Co., Limited (ZAC) is a professional company with more than 15 years experience with customized production of merino wool underwear, active wear, sportswear and accessories with genuine merino wool from New Zealand and Australia.

As a vertically integrated manufacturer, the company specializes in the production of flatlock stitch and normal stitch merino wool underwear, thermal wear and outdoor clothing.

With more than 30 Yamato flatlock (ISO 607) sewing machines, around 100 sewing workers and a floor area of more than 4500 square meters, the total capacity is more than 120,000 pcs per month.

**MOQ:** 1000 pcs per style per fabric

**Production Capacity:** 100,000 pcs per month

Merino wool underwear, thermal wear and outdoor clothing are its main products. Located in one of the largest merino wool import city of Zhangjiagang, the company uses genuine Australia merino wool in its products.

**Products:**

- merino wool base layer
- merino wool underwear
- merino wool balaclava
- merino wool printed fabrics
- merino wool solid dyed fabrics
- merino wool jacquard fabrics

**Basic styles of merino wool products:**

- Long sleeve top
- Long legging johns
- Short sleeve top
- Boxer
- Tank top
- Hoodies
- Neck warmer
- Balaclava
- Merino wool blanket
- Merino wool beanies
