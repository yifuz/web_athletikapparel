# BTEXCO Products - Customized Knit Fabric Production

BTEXCO does customized production of all types of knitted fabrics according to buyer's samples and/or technical specifications for sewing factories of underwear, sportswear and outdoor clothing.

**All Types of Knit Fabrics**

Performance fabrics for Knitwear, Underwear, Sportswear and Outdoor Clothing.

BTEXCO products include all types of performance and normal knitted fabrics including but not limited to:

- Drirelease fabrics
- Outlast fabrics
- Celliant fabrics
- Coolmax, Coolplus, Topcool, Coolpass fabrics
- UV-cut fabrics
- Cooling fabrics
- Heat generating, heat retention fabrics
- Bamboo charcoal fabrics
- Coffee charcoal fabrics
- Merino wool fabrics
- Polypropylene (polypro or pp) fabrics

*Fabrics by Functions:* Antimicrobial/Deodorant, Bamboo Charcoal, Bamboo Viscose, Drirelease, COOLDRI, Graphene, Waterproof/Windproof/Breathable, Hollow Fiber, Quick Dry/Moisture Management, Far Infrared, One-Way Moisture Transfer, Recycled Polyester, Intrinsically Deodorant, Intrinsically Antimicrobial, UV Protective.

*New Fabrics and Technology:* New Fabrics, New Product Release, Meltblown, COTTONTEK, KWIKWARM, CARBONDRY, SYNSILK, Fabrics with branded yarn.

*Fabric Collections:* 2010-2022 yearly collections + 2013/2016 Merino Wool collections + 2018 Performance Fabric collection.
