# About BTEXCO - Bulk Performance Fabric Manufacturer

Beta Textiles Co., Limited (BTEXCO), is a specialized **bulk fabric manufacturer** of all types of knitted fabrics according to buyer's samples, designs, technical specifications, concepts and/or ideas.

With its own knitting facilities located in one of the ten textiles production centers in China, with abundant resources in yarns, knitting machines, dyeing and finishing houses, the Company provides whole package fabric solutions for global garment manufacturers and fabric importers, wholesalers and traders.

The Company operates a knitting plant with more than 50 circular knitting machines and works with tens of circular knitting and warp knitting contractors to ensure most knitted fabrics can be produced according to buyer's requirements.

The annual export sales of fabrics of the Company is around **10 million USD per year**.

The Company has built a strong relationship with a great variety of yarn producers home and abroad.

**Special and strong products include:**

- Merino wool fabrics
- Polypropylene fabrics
- Bamboo charcoal fabrics
- Dri-release fabrics
- Outlast fabrics
- Nylon/spandex fabrics
- Polyester/spandex fabrics
- Cotton and cotton/spandex fabrics
- Bamboo yarn fabrics
- Modal fabrics
- and more...

BTEXCO manufactures fabrics with a great variety of branded yarns including but not limited to:

- Coolmax, Cordura, Supplex, Thermolite
- Coolpas, Coolplus
- Dri-release, Outlast, Hydropur, Celliant
- CoolVisions
- UV-cut and more

**Functions of our fabrics:**

- Moisture management (wicking and quick dry)
- Antimicrobial
- UV Protective
- Water proof
- Water repellant
- Odor-free etc.
