# Brand Partners

Performance Fabrics produced by BTEXCO have been used by its parent company Beta Textiles Co., Limited for garment production for many international sports brands. BTEXCO has also exported fabrics to Vietnam, Bangladesh, India, Cambodia, Mexico, Jordan etc for garment production.

The page is a logo wall of **~100+ brand partners**. Each logo image is in the gallery below; brand names are parsed from the source filenames (the live page shows logos without text captions).
