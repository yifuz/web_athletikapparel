# Fabric Collections

At the end of each year, BTEXCO compiles its best fabrics into fabric collections with physical swatches. These fabric collection books are designed for designers and fabric sourcing personnel in base layer, sportswear and underwear companies.

These books will be delivered by courier service free of charge to designers working in garment companies with potentials.

**Fabric Showroom**

Address:
R632, Huafang International Plaza, No.178, Chengbei Road,
Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu, China

- More than 1000 performance fabrics available by category
- Garment style samples are also available for designers
