## What we do today  (real, provable points)
================================================================

**Recycled & responsibly sourced materials**
> We offer GRS-certified (Global Recycled Standard) recycled fabrics for brands
> incorporating recycled content, and support more responsibly sourced materials
> including Better Cotton. Our fabrics are tested to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 for
> harmful substances.

**Vertical integration & traceability**
> Because we run our own fabric mill with in-house testing, we have visibility
> into the materials that go into our garments — supporting traceability and
> consistent quality from yarn to finished piece.

**Natural and performance fibers**
> Our range includes natural fibers such as merino wool alongside engineered
> performance fabrics, giving brands options that balance performance with
> material choice.

**Responsible manufacturing**
> Our production is backed by recognized social-compliance audits and
> memberships — including BSCI, Sedex, and WRAP — reflecting our commitment to
> responsible, ethical manufacturing across our operations.

**Footprint data on request**
> For brands that require it, we can support environmental footprint / LCA data
> collection for specific products as part of a project.

## Certifications & Compliance
================================================================
Split into two groups for accuracy — buyers respect the distinction between
environmental certifications and social-compliance audits. Use real badges only
(see uploaded set). NOTE: "Sears" is a retailer factory-audit relationship, NOT
a certification — do NOT list it here (it belongs on the client logo wall if
authorized). "Higg Index" is an assessment tool, not a certification — word it
as "assessed with," not "certified."

**Environmental & material certifications**
- OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 (Confidence in Textiles) — tested for harmful substances
- GRS — Global Recycled Standard (recycled content)
- Better Cotton — more sustainable cotton sourcing
- FSC® — responsible forestry (for relevant paper/packaging/fibers)

**Social compliance & responsible sourcing**
- BSCI — Business Social Compliance Initiative (participant)
- Sedex — ethical supply chain membership
- WRAP — Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production
- Higg Index — we assess our products using the Higg Index (state as
  "assessed with the Higg Index," NOT "Higg certified")
