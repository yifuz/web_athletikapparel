## What makes us different  (capability = trust)
================================================================

**Vertical Integration**
> Our own fabric mill, with full in-house testing, supplies the performance and
> thermal knits used in our garments — so quality is controlled from yarn to
> finished piece.

**Technical Construction**
> Specialized in flatlock seam and activeseam production with Yamato and Merrow
> machines, plus bonded-welded options — refined over 15+ years of technical
> knitwear.

**Performance Fabrics**
> Microfiber, merino wool, power stretch, Genesis fleece, and 4-way stretch,
> with functional finishes: moisture-wicking, UV-protective, and antimicrobial
> (including bamboo charcoal).

**Our Own Facility**
> A 4,500+ m² production facility with the capacity to handle 100,000+ pieces
> per month, scaling with your order without compromising quality or lead times.
